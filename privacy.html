Airtime

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

Airtime ("the app") is a training-tracking app for trampoline and tumbling athletes. This policy explains what happens to your data — the short version is: it never leaves your device.

Data Collection

Airtime does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app has no analytics, no advertising, and no crash reporting.

The app itself makes no network requests and has no server or backend. The one exception: the Skills section has an optional "Watch Tutorials" button that opens a YouTube search for that skill in your device's browser. This takes you outside Airtime entirely — Airtime does not embed, track, or receive anything back from that search. Once you leave the app, YouTube's own privacy policy applies, not this one.

Data Storage

All information you enter — your name, club, training sessions, competition results, skill progress, goals, and any photos or reference videos you attach — is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework and local file storage. This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is never transmitted to us or any third party
  • Is not backed up to any server we operate
  • May be included in your device's iCloud/iTunes backup if you have that enabled at the iOS level — that backup is controlled entirely by you and Apple, not by us

Third-Party Services

Airtime does not integrate with any third-party SDKs, analytics platforms, or advertising networks, and does not share any of your data with third parties. The only third-party interaction is the optional "Watch Tutorials" link described above, which opens YouTube in your browser at your request — Airtime does not send YouTube any of your data, and nothing you search there is visible to Airtime.

Children's Privacy

Airtime is designed for use by athletes of all ages, including children, under the supervision of a parent, guardian, or coach. Because no data is collected or transmitted off-device, Airtime does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Your Control

Since all data lives only in the app's local storage, you can delete all of it at any time by deleting the app from your device. There is no account to close and no server-side data to request deletion of, because none exists.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in the future (for example, if a feature that requires network access is added), this page will be updated and the app's version history will note the change.

Contact
