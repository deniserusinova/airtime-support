Common questions

Is my data backed up anywhere?
No — everything you log in Airtime stays on your device. It's only included in your iCloud/iTunes backup if you have that turned on at the iOS level.
How do I add another athlete?
Open Settings from the Dashboard tab and use "Add Athlete" to create and switch between multiple profiles.
How do I export a training log or competition results?
Use the share/export button on the Training Log or Competition detail screens to generate a PDF.
Is Airtime affiliated with USA Gymnastics?
No. Airtime is an independent, unofficial training tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by USA Gymnastics.
